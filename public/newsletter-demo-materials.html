ICT · File sizes

Put these example files in order from smallest to largest. Which two would you remove first if you needed lots of free space?

Check together

Text note → photo → song → video. Removing the example video and song would free the most space. These are example sizes: real files vary.

Talk together: Why might a video need more space than a short note?

Maths · Place value

Build 342 with drawings of hundreds, tens and ones. Compare it with 324. Which is greater?

Check together

342 = 300 + 40 + 2. Both numbers have 3 hundreds. 342 has 4 tens and 324 has 2 tens, so 342 is greater.

Try another: make a number with 5 hundreds, 1 ten and 6 ones.

Science · Light detectives

Find three objects at home. Which produce their own light, and which can you see because light shines on them?

Check together

A switched-on lamp or torch produces light. A book reflects light. The Moon reflects sunlight.

Use a torch with an adult. Never look directly at the Sun or shine a torch into anyone’s eyes.
