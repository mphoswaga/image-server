# Battle Royale duels and supporters

Surviving learners are randomly paired each round. An odd group ends with one
three-person duel so nobody sits out. A four-second VS introduction precedes the
shared 25-second question timer. Correct answers preserve lives; incorrect or
missing answers cost one of three lives. The fastest final correct submission
wins the duel, including exact ties. Fan advice does not submit game answers.

Eliminated learners select a survivor to watch and recommend diagram areas.
Only authenticated eliminated members can send support, only for the current
matchup/question and a participating survivor. Recommendations are stored per
fan per round and replace that fan's earlier recommendation. They do not alter
assessment evidence or life calculations. Players see recommendation percentages
and supporter names after ten seconds, with a pulsing button/diagram outline.
Student answer selections are not exposed before reveal. Paused and closed
rounds reject support. The next round starts with fresh recommendations.

Final rankings use lives, correct answers, then cumulative correct-response time.
Exact ties share the winning place. Teacher finish also opens the finale; an
unfinished question is not scored. The traditional Team Duels mode is unchanged.
