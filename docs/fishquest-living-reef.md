# Journey to the Living Reef

Smartboard FishQuest now uses a marine adventure for new story games. Existing version-1 sessions retain their original shark/net flow. Turning off Marine story adventure retains the classic feeding game.

The introduction automatically starts play after 20 seconds and can be skipped. Encounters occur after roughly 20%, 45%, 70% and 100% of the selected learner turns: current, shark, turtle rescue, net. Every learner still receives an equal number of question turns. Very small classes can see adjacent encounters. Encounter choices are not timed; teachers can pause any stage for discussion. Warning and result clocks survive pause/reload.

Teams start with 3 energy. Every third correct team answer offers food/growth, +3 energy (cap 12), or one scout for the next encounter. A scout opens a free safe route and is consumed after that team's encounter decision. Choices explicitly show costs; all encounters have an affordable, recoverable path. Reef Guardian awards come from helping the turtle. These classroom resources never write individual assessment marks.
