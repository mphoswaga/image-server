Teacher notes & model details

Ask learners to predict, test, and explain. Compare a file’s name, extension, type, unit and example size. Extensions give clues about format; changing an extension does not convert the file.

The activity uses the supplied lesson examples: text note 100 B, photo 600 KB, song 5 MB, video 1 GB and game 1 TB. These are teaching examples, not fixed sizes for every file of that type. Actual sizes depend on format, content, quality and compression.

This activity uses decimal units: 1 KB = 1,000 B; 1 MB = 1,000 KB; 1 GB = 1,000 MB; 1 TB = 1,000 GB. The learner unit focus is B, KB, MB, GB and TB. The 2 GB storage model lets learners test combinations that include the 1 GB video.

Everything runs offline. Quiz answers and activities reset when the page is reloaded. No accounts, downloads or internet connection are needed.
